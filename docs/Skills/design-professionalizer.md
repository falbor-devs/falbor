# Design Professionalizer

**Description:**
This skill instructs the AI to evaluate a product's design and suggest ways to elevate it to a professional, premium standard that outshines the competition. The AI must launch a browser to evaluate the site.

**Execution Context:**
- The AI must launch the browser using its available browser tools.
- It will navigate to the target site and analyze the visual aesthetic, layout, and UX.

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Visual Hierarchy & Contrast:** Does the page properly guide the user's eye? Are primary CTAs distinctly visible compared to secondary actions?
- **Color Palette Harmonization:** Is the color scheme cohesive, or does it clash? Suggestions for subtle background tints or refined brand colors.
- **Micro-interactions & Polish:** Suggestions for hover states, subtle transitions, and animations that give the site a "premium" feel.
- **Whitespace Utilization:** Ensuring elements have proper breathing room, preventing the UI from looking cluttered.
- **Modern UI Patterns:** Moving away from outdated structures (like heavy borders or rigid boxes) to modern trends (e.g., soft glassmorphism, subtle borders, expansive layouts).

**Output:**
Provide a prioritized list of design improvements. For each, explain *why* it elevates the design to a professional standard and provide actionable CSS or component-level recommendations.
